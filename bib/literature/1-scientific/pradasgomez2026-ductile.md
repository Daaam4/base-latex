# DUCTILE: Agentic LLM Orchestration of Engineering Analysis in Product Development Practice

## Topic
LLM-agent orchestration of engineering analysis when tool interfaces, data formats and documented processes keep changing.

## Method
DUCTILE (Delegated, User-supervised Coordination of Tool- and document-Integrated LLM-Enabled orchestration): the agent performs *adaptive orchestration* (interpret documented practice, inspect inputs, adapt path); verified engineering tools perform *deterministic execution*; the engineer supervises. Demonstrated on an industrial structural-analysis task at an aerospace manufacturer; evaluated against **expert-defined acceptance criteria across 10 repeated independent runs** plus deployment with practising engineers.

## Relevance
Template for (1) the agent–tool division of labour — the bridge exposes MSoSA's deterministic validation/evaluation, the agent orchestrates; (2) reliability reporting via repeated independent runs and pass^k; (3) the discussion of unintended effects (supervisory fatigue) for the human-in-the-loop framing.
