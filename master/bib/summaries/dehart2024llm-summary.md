# Leveraging Large Language Models for Direct Interaction with SysML v2

**Bib:** `dehart2024llm` · **Type:** 1 scientific · **Verified:** Crossref 15.09.2026
**Metadata:** DeHart, John K. · *INCOSE International Symposium* 34(1), pp. 2168–2185, July 2024 · DOI 10.1002/iis2.13262
**Tags:** #LLM #SysMLv2 #Conversational #Vision #AutomationBias

## Topic
The founding paper of the "LLM ↔ SysML v2" topic (cited by SysForge, SysTemp, Fresemann et al.). Argues that SysML v2's English-like textual syntax and the parallel maturation of LLMs make *conversational* interaction with system models feasible, and that the LLM can act as an interpretive layer replacing API expertise for model manipulation.

## Method
Conceptual analysis plus case studies; no controlled evaluation. Explicitly names the pitfalls: automation bias, overreliance, need for human oversight.

## Relevance
Provides the vision the thesis puts to an empirical test. Its claim that LLMs "reduce dependency on technical expertise for API navigation" is exactly what an MCP bridge operationalises — and what the capability evaluation measures. Cite as the origin of the idea; contrast its absence of measurement with the thesis's benchmark.
