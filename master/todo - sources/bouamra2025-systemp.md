# SysTemp: A Multi-Agent System for Template-Based Generation of SysML v2

**Bib:** `bouamra2025systemp` · **Type:** 1 scientific (preprint) · **Verified:** arXiv 15.09.2026
**Metadata:** Bouamra, Yasmine; Yun, Bruno; Poisson, Alexandre; Armetta, Frédéric · arXiv:2506.21608 [cs.AI], June 2025
**Tags:** #MultiAgent #Templates #Generation #Gap

## Topic
Automatic generation of SysML v2 from natural-language specifications, motivated by the scarcity of training corpora and the complex syntax.

## Method
Multi-agent system with a *template generator* agent that structures the generation before the model text is produced. Evaluation discussed qualitatively; the paper states that no semantic evaluation method and no benchmark exist for SysML v2 generation.

## Relevance
**The gap citation.** The thesis's benchmark with ground truth and expert acceptance criteria is precisely what SysTemp says is missing. Also a data point that the field has moved to multi-agent generation while nobody evaluates interactive, tool-using agents.
